一、理论基础与分析框架的提出（一）理论基础生成式人工智能内容的生成、传播及其影响并非由单一主体完成，而是模型提供者、平台运营者、内容使用者等多元主体在不同环节共同作用的结果。并且，生成式人工智能内容风险往往具有生成速度快、传播范围广且社会影响逆转难等特征，其生成和演化呈现一个环环相扣的链式结构，任何环节的治理效果都会直接影响到治理效能［14］。因此，在生成式人工智能情境下，内容治理责任不再是静态归属问题，而表现为一种嵌入特定信息结构中的系统性问题，有必要引入能够刻画多元要素互动关系的整体性分析框架。本文引入信息生态系统理论作为分析视角，该理论最早由美国学者托马斯·达文波特（Thomas Davenport）和劳伦普·普鲁萨克（Laurence Prusak）于1997 年提出［15］。信息生态系统理论从生态学的视角切入，将信息视为一种生态系统，是信息自身与生命体及其周围环境相互联系、相互作用的有机整体，包括信息、信息人、信息环境三大基本要素［16］。具体而言，信息是生态系统的核心要素，是信息生态的客体，其生成、传播和使用方式直接影响整个信息生态系统的运行状态［17］。信息人是生态系统的主体要素，指在信息

Review and Configuration of Content Governance Responsibilities for Generative Artificial Intelligence WANG Guang-hui PENG Xin-qiao the production methods, dissemination logics, and governance conditions of information content, rendering the issue of content governance responsibility unprecedentedly complex. Specifically, content generation has shifted from being led by a single subject to a composite model characterized by human-machine collaboration and the participation of multiple actors. Content risks have expanded from individual illegal or non-compliant content to systemic risks that run through the entire process of generation, distribution, and dissemination. Furthermore, the technological architecture, platform mechanisms, and institutional rules upon which responsibility implementation depends have also undergone profound changes. Consequently, the traditional logic of responsibility, based on stable subjects, clear objects, and post-facto attribution, has been challenged, giving rise to three responsibility dilemmas: blurred responsibility attribution amidst the participation of multiple actors, difficulties in responsibility determination amidst the escalation of risk patterns, and impediments to responsibility operation amidst environmental complexity. To address these challenges, it is necessary to construct a whole-process, multi-actor responsibility allocation mechanism, improve a risk-oriented tiered and categorized governance responsibility system, and promote the embedding of responsibility requirements into technological architecture and institutional rules.
