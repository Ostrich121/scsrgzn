（责任编辑：徐然）Understanding Crimes Involving Generative AI：Perspectives and Criminal Law Responses

Abstract：The surge of Generative Artificial Intelligence，while reshaping the paradigms of information

processing，also poses a significant challenge to criminal governance. Existing research tends to either indulge in excessive speculation about future strong AI or mechanically apply established theories when addressing crimes involving weak AI，thereby failing to accurately grasp the evolution of risks enabled by this technology. There is an urgent need to develop a more robust macroscopic framework for understanding，grounded in the generational evolution of cybercrime. Accordingly，generative AI is easily used as an advanced enabling tool that accelerates the evolution of cybercrime towards Web 3. 0. Its realworld risks can be typologized into four core forms：instrumental risks，target-based risks，negligencerelated risks，and derivative risks. To effectively mitigate these risks，a tiered risk prevention and control framework should be established at the meso level of risk regulation，and the boundaries of criminal law intervention must be prudently delineated. At the micro level of criminal law response，theoretical adjustments to the principles of attribution are required to address the challenges posed by generative AI. Furthermore，typified paths for attribution should be specifically explored for diverse actors—such as developers，users，and platform service providers—based on their respective roles and control capabilities within the risk chain.
